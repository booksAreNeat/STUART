Our Goal

We aim to create a prescription tracking and management software to be utilised by private healthcare practices

This software will be accessed by patients through a mobile app that will allow them to see the constant whereabouts and status of their prescriptions, and provide them with an accurate timeframe for when they will be able to collect their medication from their pharmacy

The app will notify the patient if there are any delays in the process and provide the contact information of the company currently handling their prescriptions, allowing them to reach out regarding delays

Our Promise

Create a pathway for you to hold companies accountable for unecessary delays

Regular updates about the present location of your prescriptions and who is currently responsible for handling it

Remove the stress from the prescription process

Streamline the prescription collection and payment system
